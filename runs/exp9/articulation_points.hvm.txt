// t3_articulation_points (GRAPH-SWING-2, runs/exp9). v (degree >= 2) is a cut vertex iff some neighbour of v is not
// reachable from a0(v) = min neighbour of v in G - v. All n of these searches run at once as ONE bitset BFS
// (msbfs template): search v is bit v, seeded at a0(v), and vertex u starts with R_u = {u}, so bit u never enters or
// leaves u (u is deleted from its own search). At the end the cut set is OR_u (Nb_u & ~R_u) (Nb_u = u's neighbour
// bitset row), listed in ascending order.

  & n ~ ?((@ap_zero @ap_pos) (es out))

  & L ~ {L1 {L2 {L3 {L4 {L5 {L6 {L7 {L8 L9}}}}}}}}
  & W ~ {W1 {W2 {W3 {W4 {W5 W6}}}}}
  & @zl ~ (L1 G0)

  & @w_blk ~ (es (((L4 W2) (G0 (A0 M0))) (G (A M))))

// zs1(L, W, base) -> (state trie of ({u} (0 0)), gather trie of (0 0))
@zs1 = (L (W (base (s g))))

// seed(A, L, E = (Lf W), base, T -> T'): for every v with a0 = A[v] < INF, OR {v} into ONES of gather leaf a0
