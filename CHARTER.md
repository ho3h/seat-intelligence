# Genome charter (from the PRD, Sep 28 2026)

Every gate report opens with the two tables below, each row marked held or broken with evidence.

## The six pillars

| Pillar | Held by | How it is measured | A violation looks like |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| 1. The graph is the program | Physics | Every answer comes from the executor reducing the net; no decoder | A net used as storage; a model that reads the net and answers |
| 2. Free of human understanding | Writing | No human language anywhere above the net; freedom diagnostic on what gets written | Authoring through Bend or any human language in the primary arm; a hand-written teacher; a human-named vocabulary |
| 3. Not serialized | Physics | Execution happens on the net; serialization only at the interface, a declared trade until the pen lands | Text or tokens as the medium of execution |
| 4. Foundational | Author at the edge | Frozen general models write and read; the runtime is the substrate | A bespoke model co-trained per task; fine-tuning as the headline |
| 5. Bare metal, simple | Physics | Lafont's combinators plus HVM2's small fixed extensions, nothing learned | Learned or task-specific rules in the executor |
| 6. Graphs all the way down | Physics | Program, data and execution state are one net | Topology or state held in a side table |

## Swing discipline (nine rules)

1. The swing is fixed on day one. No revision may shrink the end state, the corpus, or the workload.
2. No smaller targets as a fix. A missed gate changes the design, never the size of the problem.
3. Every phase is at least 10x the last, in programs, program size or input size. Nothing passes or is reported on toy sizes.
4. Small tests are instruments, not milestones. Phase 0 can end the work; it is never progress.
5. No phase ends "promising." Each ends in a pass, one written revision, or a stop.
6. Lanes run in parallel. The swing workload is wired up from week 1.
7. A fixed calendar. 14 weeks to the swing. A slip costs weeks, never scope.
8. Rigor lives in the verifier. Every program is run and checked at full size; caution is more checks, never a smaller task.
9. Charter first. Every gate report confirms the above.

## The swing (fixed)

By week 14 the reconciliation core of Orbweaver runs as a machine-authored Genome net, in a vocabulary no human defined,
correct on replayed real graphs, faster than the best human-language route (B1, B2) at 100,000 nodes or more on CUDA,
across a 200-program corpus (T1 30, T2 30, T3 50, T4 40, T5 50) with three authoring seeds.
